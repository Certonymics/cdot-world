Mission

The settlement layer for value exchanged between people, businesses, and machines.

Rather than forcing everything onto a single chain, cDot is a family of purpose built blockchains, each engineered for what it does best: identity, stablecoin settlement, and agentic transactions, interoperating as one trusted network.

The problem we're solving

Stablecoins enable instant, borderless, programmable transactions. Today's blockchain infrastructure isn't designed for them.

Existing systems are either fully general or optimized for trading, and none treat identity as a first-class primitive.

Managing identity by only proving you are a human reaches its limit when it comes to payments and regulation. cDot introduces privacy with accountability: cryptographic avatars of the user's real identity.

This gap becomes critical as AI agents begin transacting on behalf of humans and businesses: without native identity and authorization, agents cannot be held accountable, and their payments cannot be trusted or audited.

Payments at scale don't just need speed and low cost; they need to know, verifiably, who or what is on each side of a transaction.

The network
